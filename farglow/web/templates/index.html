Every star counts

Farglow is a grounded sci-fi 4X played in about five minutes a day. Guide a young civilization out among real-scale stars. The galaxy moves on an hourly clock whether you're watching or not, and each morning the Dispatch tells you what happened.

This server is running First Light, the first playable proof of concept: one civilization, the economy, research, surveys, outposts, colonies, buildings, the advisor and the Dispatch.
