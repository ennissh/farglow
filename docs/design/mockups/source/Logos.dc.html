Brand · exploration

Farglow logo concepts

Earthrise, retold with two or three suns. Each mark shown in light and dark, at hero size, app-icon sizes and favicon size, and as a lockup with the wordmark.
Gold = the brand glow (logo only, not a UI colour). Blue companion = the "you" accent, a nod to "every star counts". Run a trademark search on the chosen mark before finalising.
